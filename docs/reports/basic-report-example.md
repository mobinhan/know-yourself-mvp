- Which commitments still feel meaningful after the initial excitement?
- What kinds of challenges are worth your effort—and which are not?
- When progress slows, what helps you recognize a useful constraint versus a signal to change approach?
- Which experiences have changed how you understand or communicate an idea?

## 5. Your Starting Point

For the coming week, choose one commitment or decision that matters to you. Once a day, take a moment to note:

1. **What happened?** Record the situation without immediately explaining it.
2. **What did you notice?** Describe your actual experience, not what you think you should have felt.
3. **What mattered?** Ask whether the situation served something meaningful to you.
4. **What will you observe next?** Choose one small question to carry into the next day.

Use this as an experiment in self-observation. Your chart can provide a language for reflection; it should not replace your judgment or make decisions for you.
