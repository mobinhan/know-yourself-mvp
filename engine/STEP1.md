# Step 1 — Deterministic Human Design Chart Engine

Step 1 now has an isolated ephemeris layer plus the structural derivation layer.

The ephemeris layer converts local birth time to UTC, calculates planetary positions with Swiss Ephemeris, uses the true lunar node, derives Earth as the solar opposite, finds the Design timestamp from the 88-degree solar displacement, and maps longitudes to Human Design gates and lines.

The structural layer then derives gates, channels, centres, definition, type, authority, profile and Incarnation Cross.

Golden Chart #2 is the acceptance test: 15 April 1982, 07:38, Baarn, Netherlands. The expected activations are stored in golden-chart.json.

No AI interpretation is part of Step 1.
